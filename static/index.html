Frequently Asked Questions

What is VideoTextScripter (video-textscripter)?
VideoTextScripter (also written as video-textscripter) is a free AI-powered online tool that converts any video into accurate text. Paste a YouTube, Instagram, or any video URL — or upload an MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, or WEBM file — and get a full transcript in seconds using OpenAI Whisper AI. No sign-up or account required.
How do I convert a YouTube video to text for free?
Paste the YouTube link into the URL field on this page, select a Whisper model, then click Get Transcript. The full text appears in seconds — no sign-up, no fees, no software to install.
What video formats are supported?
VideoTextScripter supports file uploads in MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, and WEBM formats. It also accepts direct URLs from YouTube, Instagram, and most other video-hosting platforms.
Is VideoTextScripter completely free?
Yes — 100% free. No subscriptions, no hidden costs, and no account needed. Open the site and start transcribing immediately.
How accurate is the transcription?
Powered by OpenAI Whisper, accuracy is excellent for clear speech — typically 95%+ for lectures, interviews, and meetings. Use the Large model for maximum precision on complex or accented audio, or Base for the best speed-accuracy balance.
Which AI model is used for transcription?
VideoTextScripter uses OpenAI Whisper — a state-of-the-art open-source speech recognition model. Five sizes are available: Tiny (fastest), Base (default), Small, Medium, and Large (most accurate).
Is it a good free alternative to Otter.ai or Descript?
Yes. VideoTextScripter is a top free alternative to Otter.ai, Descript, Rev, and Sonix. Unlike those paid tools, it's completely free, requires no sign-up, and supports both URL-based and file-upload transcription using OpenAI Whisper directly in your browser.
